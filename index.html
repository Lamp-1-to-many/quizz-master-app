Welcome to QuizMaster

Your comprehensive guide to mastering the platform, building an effective question bank, and securing your data.

What is QuizMaster?

In simple terms, QuizMaster is your personal, highly secure offline testing platform. Instead of creating fixed "quizzes," you build a single massive Question Bank. The app then acts like a smart tutor, letting you pull from this bank to practice casually or take strict, timed exams.

Everything runs directly in your browser. No internet is required once the app is loaded, and your data never leaves your device.

How It Works (Step-by-Step)

1

Build Your Bank

Go to Add Questions to type them in manually, or use the Manage Bank tab to import hundreds of questions at once using a CSV spreadsheet. Questions are automatically sorted by Subject and Topic.

2

Practice & Learn

Go to the Practice tab, pick a topic, and start answering. It's stress-free: there is no timer, no negative marking, and you get instant feedback and explanations the moment you click an option.

3

Take an Exam

Ready for the real deal? Go to Exam Mode. You choose how many questions and how much time you want. The app will strictly enforce a countdown timer and apply negative marking (+3 for correct, -1 for wrong, 0 for skipped).

Import & Export Data

You don't have to type questions one by one. You can use Excel or Google Sheets to create your question bank and import it in one click via the Manage Bank tab.

CSV Required Header Format (First Row):

Subject, Topic, Question Text, Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, Correct Answer (1-5), Explanation
  • Correct Answer: Must be a number. If Option 2 is correct, type 2.
  • Empty Fields: If a question only has 3 options, just leave the Option 4 and 5 columns blank. Explanations are also optional.
  • Exporting: Click Export CSV at any time to download a backup spreadsheet of your entire encrypted bank.

Security & Privacy

Your data privacy and test integrity are prioritized. Even though data is stored on your device, it can be locked down.

  • AES Encryption: Set a master password via the lock icon in the top right. Your local bank will be fully encrypted (AES) making it unreadable without the password.
  • High-Capacity Storage: Uses IndexedDB technology, allowing you to securely store tens of thousands of questions directly on your device without hitting typical browser limits.
  • Exam Anti-Cheat: When Exam Mode is active, standard right-click menus and text-copy functions are automatically disabled to simulate a strict testing environment.
